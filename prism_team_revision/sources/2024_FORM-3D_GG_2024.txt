         FLASH Effect in proton therapy:

         Problem: modern PBS proton therapy centers (PTC)
         can’t deliver UHDR due to the energy switching
         time required between different energy layers (0.5-
         2 s)

                  The FLASH effect is lost

          3D Range Modulators (3DRM)

          Solution: 3DRM, objects able to passively modulate
          the proton pencil beam energy and conformally
          shape the dose in the target (tumor)

 OUTLOOK

        FORM-3D aims to make a concrete step towards the translation of FLASH proton therapy into
        clincs, in the framework of the PTC of Trento, to be possibly extended to other facilities

                                                       (but not only)

        A part from Flash Radiotherapy, the project will be
        important also:

        • To speed up the current PT treatments, with an
          impact in the treatment of moving targets (e.g.
          Lung) and to better allocate the PTC resources
        • Create the basis for the use of FRED as an
          independent MC tool at the PTC of Trento for
          further research
        • Fast calculation of dose rate metrics with
          biophysical implications
        • Contribute to the development of FRED, due to a
          ongoing collaboration
        • Possible technological transfer
