Phase 1: Single module design
A small HTS racetrack coil design aiming at one
ECHO module will be performed.
The design must assess the following question:

• Realize a solid design with operational margins

• Achieve a repeatible field quality

• Optimize resistive joints

• Handle the assembly on a curved support

• Set up a QA procedure in LN2
       Modularity and repeatibility of the process

• Given the expertise gained from INFN-led project as SIG e HITRI+ on SC gantry,
  a classical CCT design was adapted to be realized in a modular structure:
      The goal is to realize a small-scale HTS dipole magnet demonstrator
       consisting of 10 modules for each layer.
• The project sets the first milestone towards a next generation of ion gantries
  introducing:
      • Energy saving.
      • Industrial scalability.

Thickness Inhomogeneity

Tapes may show thickness inhomogeneity
commonly referred to as “dog-bone”, occurring
during the electroplating of the final copper layer.

This may lead to:

• Localized stresses

• Winding issues
