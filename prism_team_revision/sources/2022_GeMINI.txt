                                          Project’s goal
       • Germanium detectors, particularly coaxial High Purity Germanium
         (HPGe) detectors, are frequently used in fundamental science and
         societal applications, thanks to their unparalleled energy resolution
         and high detection e ciency in a broad energy range.

       • Typical monolithic HPGe detectors lack however the ability to
         measure the direction of the incident radiation

       • Incident gamma direction is crucial in various elds, including
         nuclear & atomic physics, dark matter, environmental and medical
         physics

GeMINI’s goal: develop, characterize and deploy a cost-e ective
real-time high-rate ML technique with enhanced performance for
estimating the direction of gamma radiation implemented on a
HPGe detector system, signi cantly extending the physics and
technological reach of these detectors. This will be achieved by a
precise study and characterization of the detector’s pulse shapes
and real-time ML on FPGA.

                               Concluding Remarks
    • GeMINI develops and innovative ultra fast on the edge ML approach
      on FGPA boards to unlock the true capabilities of HPGe detectors
    • To achieve this, we leverage the best of two worlds: HEP and
      Underground physics
    • As PI, I have years of experience in algorithm design, machine learning
      data analysis, as well as team leading and managerial skills;
      GeMINI can boost my future career
    • GeMINI introduces a new technology, path nder to deeper understating
      of these excellent detectors, inside INFN and in concert with world-
      leading institution (University of Zurich and University of Zagreb)
    • GeMINI will realize a demonstration through a preliminary measurement
      with radioactive sources and at the LNF accelerator complex (DAΦNE/
      BTF), with high scienti c impact
    • Within GeMINI, a wealth of experimental e orts will be able to bene t a
      great increase in sensitivity at a low cost
