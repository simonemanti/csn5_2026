● Improved Alignment: Feasibility study optimizing crystal Rotation and Tilting for the Iron Kα

● Faster Convergence: Bayesian Optimization achieves convergence in just 20 iterations,

Risks Assessment
Inaccuracy of Ray-Tracing Simulations (T1.3)
 ●   Risk: Simulations may not fully represent the XES experiment due to limitations in modeling mosaic crystals.
 ●   Mitigation: Collaborate with SHADOW-XOP developer Manuel Sanchez del Rio to enhance simulation accuracy.

Data Integration and Feature Extraction Issues (T1.5)
 ●   Risk: Challenges in integrating data and extracting XES spectral features with deep learning.
 ●   Mitigation: Leverage historical data to align simulated and real-world data.

Delays in Equipment Integration (T2.1, T2.2, T2.3)
 ●   Risk: Delays in acquiring or integrating new equipment could impact timelines.
 ●   Mitigation: Use a VOXES CCD as a temporary solution until the pixel detector is ready.

Optimization Complexity (T3.1, T3.2)
 ●   Risk: Multi-objective optimization (e.g., time, precision, resolution) is complex and computationally demanding.
 ●   Mitigation: Adjust strategies based on results and consult experts like Lucio Anderlini for AI-driven enhancements.
