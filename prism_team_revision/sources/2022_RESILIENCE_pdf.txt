 Conclusions and perspectives
• RESILIENCE aims to fabricate and test a magnetic field resilient microwave single photon detector.

• To achieve this goal, we plan to assembly JJ using the Ising superconductor NbSe2. In this way the JJ
  inherits the magnetic field resistance of the NbSe2.

• Optimization of the magnetic field resiliency, through the optimization of the assembly parameters, is
  crucial. We will test the most promising device as a single photon detector , down to 8 mK and in presence
  of 9 T magnetic field

• vdW materials are the perfect platform for quantum technologies and they are being more and more used
  to fabricate quantum circuits. Developing the know-how and the instrumentations necessary to build all-
  vdW quantum components will be extremely rewarding for the future of INFN in the field of quantum
  technologies
