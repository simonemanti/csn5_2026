Conclusions
PHASE-g aims at validating a novel approach to perovskite-based γ-ray
detectors, which merges the bulk properties of Single Crystals with the
major advantages of interdigitated structures.
It provides:
➡   An Innovative dual structure which relies on 3D interdigitated
electrodes
➡   A dedicated study on PMPS-based scintillating device for photon
counting
➡➡  A spectroscopic device for γ-rays of different doses and energies
    Tests with clinically relevant radiation energies
It opens up new paradigms for detectors fabrication and thus scalability to
large-area, pixelated multielectrode arrays.
